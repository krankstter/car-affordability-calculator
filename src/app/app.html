Estimates only, for educational purposes, not financial advice. Built on commonly cited rules of thumb: EMI ≤ 20% of monthly income, total car cost ≤ 10–15% of income, total committed outflow (EMI + existing debt + SIPs) ≤ 40% of income, the "20/4/10" rule (20% down, ≤4-year loan, ≤10% of income), insurance ≈ 2.25% of on-road value/year (tapering down year-over-year with your depreciation rate in the multi-year projection), maintenance ≈ 1.5% of price/year rising ~8%/yr with age, and 10–15%/year declining-balance depreciation. Actual figures vary by state, insurer, model and usage. Confirm with your dealer, bank and insurer before deciding.
